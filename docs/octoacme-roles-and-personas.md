# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers drive user experience quality by working with Product Managers, Developers, and QA to translate business goals into usable, intuitive interfaces.

### Responsibilities
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and visual specifications
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Provide design assets and guidance to developers during implementation
- Collaborate with Product and Engineering to ensure user needs are met

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce friction and support adoption of new features
- Ensure design consistency across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with Product and Engineering
- Handoff documentation (Figma, design specs) to developers
- Usability findings shared with Product Managers and QA

### Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on feature definition, user feedback, and prioritization
- **Developers**: Deliver design specs and collaborate on feasibility and implementation
- **QA**: Review completed work for usability and alignment with design intent

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts enable data-driven decisions by analyzing product and process metrics to generate actionable insights for the team.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics in collaboration with Product and Project Managers
- Build and maintain dashboards to track project and product progress
- Analyze user behavior and business impact of shipped features
- Flag anomalies and suggest optimizations based on data findings

### Goals
- Ensure decisions are grounded in evidence and measurable outcomes
- Surface insights that improve product quality and team efficiency
- Make data accessible and understandable across the team

### Typical Communication
- Dashboard summaries shared with Product and Project Managers
- Data findings presented in sprint reviews or planning sessions
- Ad-hoc analysis reports as needed

### Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on defining and tracking success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Provide progress and velocity data for status reporting
- **Developers**: Coordinate on telemetry instrumentation and data availability

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee the delivery of releases, ensuring quality and adherence to process for all production deployments.

### Responsibilities
- Own the deployment calendar and release checklists
- Coordinate cross-functional release readiness (engineering, QA, support)
- Facilitate go/no-go decisions and manage rollback plans if needed
- Ensure release notes and communications are prepared before deployment

### Goals
- Deliver releases safely, predictably, and with minimal disruption
- Maintain a clear and auditable release history
- Reduce release risk through rigorous pre-deployment checks

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness updates to PM, PdM, and Support
- Go/no-go meeting facilitation with engineering and QA leads
- Post-release retrospective notes and incident summaries

### Interactions
- **Developers/QA**: Coordinate on release candidates, validation, and sign-off
- **Product/Project Managers**: Align on release priorities and timelines
- **Support/Customer Success**: Provide release details for customer communications

---

## Support / Customer Success

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success teams serve as the voice of the customer post-release, ensuring prompt resolution of user-reported issues and closing the feedback loop with the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate customer issues and feedback to Product and Project Managers
- Help prioritize hotfixes based on customer impact and severity
- Provide release communications and usage updates to customers
- Document recurring issues and patterns for product improvement

### Goals
- Ensure customers are informed and supported through every release
- Reduce time-to-resolution for user-reported issues
- Feed customer insights back into the product roadmap

### Typical Communication
- Issue and feedback summaries shared with Product and Project Managers
- Release notes and announcements delivered to customers
- Escalation paths coordinated with the Release Manager and Developers

### Interactions
- **Product/Project Managers**: Share customer feedback and help prioritize fixes
- **Release Manager**: Coordinate on release communications and incident response
- **Developers**: Provide detailed bug reports and reproduction steps
